Task Description

The task objective will be accomplished by measuring the thermal conductivity of liquid helium confined in cylindrical tubes of diameters from 1 to 100 microns. The objective of BEST is to determine the effects of boundaries on the thermal conductivity of liquid helium-4 near the superfluid transition The result of this work will provide the first test of scaling for transport properties near a continuous phase transition.

Research Impact & Earth Benefits

A comparison between experimental results and theoretical prdictions will enhance our ability to calculate properties of complex systems using renormalization-group and Monte Carlo methods.

Task Progress Report

Thermal-conductivity measurements were made on plates with channels of 1x10 micro-m cross section, and simultaneously on bulk helium. The bulk measurements served to provide the bulk lambda-point as a fixed point on the temperature scale. Analysis of the data is now complete and a publication has been submitted to the Journal of Low Temperature Physics. To our great surprise the results show that the finite-size scaling-function for this geometry does not differ measurably from the scaling function for cylindrical confinement. This differs qualitatively from what had been found for thermodynamic properties in one-dimensional and two-dimensional confinement.
